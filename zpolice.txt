   2  0000              ; Z-POLICE  -  3D vector city flight for the ZX Spectrum Next
   3  0000              ;
   4  0000              ;  Z80N assembler (sjasmplus --zxnext).  Layer 2 256x192x8bpp, double buffered.
   5  0000              ;  Maths: world positions are 16-bit integers (one city cell = 256 units),
   6  0000              ;         trig values are S7.8 fixed point (1.0 = $0100).
   7  0000              ;  Hidden line removal: painter's algorithm. Buildings are walked over the
   8  0000              ;  grid far-to-near (no sort needed). Each building is filled solid and then
   9  0000              ;  outlined, so nearer buildings overwrite the lines of those behind them:
  10  0000              ;    - wholly on screen: its silhouette (table driven from the visible face
  11  0000              ;      set) is filled once, outlined, and the interior edges drawn as lines
  12  0000              ;    - crossing the view frustum: each visible face is clipped in 3D
  13  0000              ;      (Sutherland-Hodgman, 5 planes), filled and outlined separately
  14  0000              ;  Rasterisation then only ever sees on-screen 8-bit coordinates.
  15  0000              ;
  16  0000              ;  Controls: Q/A speed up/down   W stop      S/F turn left/right
  17  0000              ;            E/D nose down/up    T/G climb/descend

  19  0000              ;  Memory while running:
