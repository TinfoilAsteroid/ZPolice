   2  0000              ; Z-POLICE  -  3D vector city flight for the ZX Spectrum Next
   3  0000              ;
   4  0000              ;  Z80N assembler (sjasmplus --zxnext).  Layer 2 256x192x8bpp, double buffered.
   5  0000              ;  Maths: world positions are 16-bit integers (one city cell = 256 units),
   6  0000              ;         trig values are S7.8 fixed point (1.0 = $0100).
   7  0000              ;  Hidden line removal: painter's algorithm. Buildings are walked over the
   8  0000              ;  grid far-to-near (no sort needed). Each visible face is filled in its
   9  0000              ;  shade (roof lighter, side turned away darker) and optionally outlined, so
  10  0000              ;  nearer buildings overwrite those behind them. Faces with every corner on
  11  0000              ;  screen use cached projections; faces crossing the view frustum are
  12  0000              ;  clipped in 3D (Sutherland-Hodgman, 5 planes). Rasterisation then only
  13  0000              ;  ever sees on-screen 8-bit coordinates.
  14  0000              ;
  15  0000              ;  Controls: Q/A speed up/down   W stop      S/F turn left/right
  16  0000              ;            E/D nose down/up    T/G climb/descend
  17  0000              ;
  18  0000              ;  Memory while running:
  19  0000              ;    $0000-$BFFF  Layer 2 back buffer (MMU slots 0-5), address = y*256+x
  20  0000              ;    $C000-$FFFF  code, tables, variables, stack (16K bank 0)
  21  0000              ;==============================================================================
