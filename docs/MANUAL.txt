Z-POLICE
========
Manual

A 3D flight game for the ZX Spectrum Next.

The dead are walking the streets. Fly your VTOL over the city, rescue the
survivors and bring them home, and stop the monsters reaching your landing
pad. There are 10 cities and 50 levels between you and safety.


RUNNING THE GAME
----------------

On a Spectrum Next: copy this whole folder to the SD card, and open
zpolice.nex from the browser. The ZPDATA folder has to stay beside it.

In the CSpect emulator: run it from inside this folder, with the ZPDATA folder
beside zpolice.nex.


MAIN MENU
---------

1  New game
2  Continue the saved game
9  Redefine keys


THE CONTROLS
------------

Flying

  Q  speed up                 A  slow down / reverse
  O  turn left                P  turn right
  W  climb                    S  descend
  E  nose up                  D  nose down
  Z  air brake

  Tap O or P for a fine correction; hold them for a full turn. The nose levels
  itself when you let go of E or D.

Weapons

  SPACE  fire the selected weapon (hold)
  1-5    select a weapon slot
  R / F  tilt the centre gun up / down
  V      recentre the centre gun

Upgrades, once you've earned them

  X      booster on / off
  N      next vision mode
  K / L  strafe left / right (hold)

Other

  BREAK  pause. Then S saves and quits, and X quits without saving.

Joysticks: Kempston and Mega Drive pads work, set up in the Next's own
joystick settings. Every control can be given any key or joystick input on
the redefine keys screen.


HOW TO PLAY
-----------

Each level is one or more waves of monsters. Wipe out the last wave and the
level is complete.

Your two jobs are:

  Rescue the humans. Land near one, and it will run to you and climb in.

  Protect the base. The monsters want your landing pad, in the middle of the
  city, and they'll break through the terrace round it to reach it.

The game is over if the landing pad is destroyed, or if your VTOL is.

Rescuing humans

  * You're landed when you're stopped and resting on the road, a roof or the
    pad. Descend until you touch down, and any speed is braked away.
  * Humans within 20 metres run to you, if they're at the same height: a human
    on a roof can't reach you on the road, nor one on the road reach you on a
    roof.
  * You can carry 5 at a time.
  * Land on the pad and they get off, one a second. Each one is saved. Anyone
    still aboard when the level ends counts too.
  * Humans on the road run from monsters. Humans on roofs can't.
  * You can't fire while you're landed.

The landing pad

Land on the pad to repair the VTOL, reload the pylons and refuel the booster.
It takes time, so you're on the ground while the monsters close in. Landing
anywhere else does none of this.

Between levels

After each level you're told how many humans you saved. Your ammunition is
replenished, and then you can change your weapons on the load-out screen.


THE SCREEN
----------

  Top of the screen     The compass. The white pointer marks your heading.
  Left of the screen    A row of icons, one for each human.
  Middle                The view, with the gunsight.
  Bottom                The instrument panel.

The human icons

  Empty square          waiting in the city
  Seated person         aboard the VTOL
  Green person          rescued
  Crossed-out person    dead

The instrument panel, from left to right

  S          Speed. Red when reversing, amber while boosting.
  A          Altitude. The left half is your height (green). The right half
             is the height of what's below you (amber). The gap between them
             is your clearance.
  B          Booster fuel, once the booster is fitted.
  Weapons    The five slots, in the order 2 4 1 5 3: see below.
  INTEGRITY  The VTOL's condition. Green, then amber, then red.
  Map        The whole city.

Each weapon slot shows its number, its ammunition reading down (INF means
infinite) and its weapon's letter. The bar is green, or red when it's out of
ammunition. The selected slot is marked in yellow. An unused slot is black.

The city map

  White             the landing pad
  Flashing          you
  Flashing blue     a human
  Green             a zombie or ghoul
  White, grey       a ghost
  Yellow            a banshee
  Flashing red      a lich
  Red               a monster attacking


THE MONSTERS
------------

Zombies    Walk the streets towards the pad, and batter anything in the way.
           Some are faster than others. On later levels there are more of
           them, and they head for the pad more directly.
Ghouls     Half the height of a zombie, and five times as fast.
Ghosts     Fly over buildings at any height, and hunt humans and the VTOL
           when they can see them. They can't hurt the base. Fading away, a
           ghost is already dead.
Banshees   Faster, deadlier ghosts, which also attack the base.
Liches     A city's boss, in the last wave of its last level (every 5th
           level). Twice a zombie's height and very tough, with fireballs
           that reach 200 metres. Each city's is harder than the last.

A building between you and a ghost or banshee hides you from it.


WEAPONS
-------

The centre slot, key 1, always holds a centre gun, which never runs out. Its
sight moves as you tilt it (R, F), 15 degrees up and 45 degrees down.

  Letter  Weapon            Notes
  M       Machine gun       Steady and hard-hitting. You start with this.
  L       Pulse laser       A beam, in short bursts.
  C       Chain gun         Very fast, weaker rounds, with some spread.

The pylons, keys 2 to 5, always fire straight ahead, and their ammunition is
limited.

  Letter  Weapon            Load  Notes
  R       Rocket pod        12    A blast that hits everything near it.
  S       Missile pod       4     Slower than a rocket, with a bigger reach.
  A       Autocannon        50    Slow-firing bursting shells.
  P       Proximity         50    Shells that burst when they pass a monster.
  B       Bomb              1     Glides down ahead of you. The largest blast
                                  of all, and it can hit you too.

Linked chain guns: a pair, the L for slot 4 only and the R for slot 5 only.
200 rounds each. With both fitted, selecting either one fires them together,
each with its own crosshair. One runs dry and the other fires on alone.


THE LOAD-OUT SCREEN
-------------------

The store is on the left, and your five slots on the right: a plan of the
VTOL's underside shows where each one is (1 the centre gun, 2 and 3 the
outer wing pods, 4 and 5 the inner ones), the selected slot in yellow, with
what is fitted in each listed below it.

  1-5    select a slot
  Q / A  move up and down the store. Green means the weapon fits that slot.
  SPACE  fit the weapon (whatever was there goes back in the store)
  U      unload the slot
  0      start the level

Slot 1 has to have a weapon in it before you can start.


SALVAGE
-------

Each human saved earns 1 salvage point at the end of the level. Your total
carries on from level to level, and reaching these totals earns the rewards.
Weapons go into the store. Everything else is fitted at once.

   10  Pulse laser                         60  Rocket pod
   15  Full repair                         65  Night vision - monochrome
   20  Chain gun                           70  Proximity cannon, full repair
   25  Night vision - green                75  Proximity cannon
   30  Rocket pod                          80  Rocket pod
   35  Vertical thrusters                  85  Thermal vision
   40  Bomb                                90  Lateral thrusters
   45  Booster module                     100  Rocket pod, full repair
   50  Linked chain gun L                 105  Autocannon, full repair
   55  Linked chain gun R, full repair    110  Autocannon, full repair

  115  Missile pod, full repair           130  Missile pod, full repair
  120  Autocannon, full repair            135  Missile pod, full repair
  125  Autocannon, full repair            140  Missile pod, full repair

From 145 on, every 5 points is a full repair.

Upgrades

  Vertical thrusters  Climb to 30 metres instead of 15.
  Booster module      X doubles your top speed, burning fuel, from at least
                      half speed. Q, A and Z do nothing while it's on. Press X
                      again to go back to your old speed.
  Lateral thrusters   K and L slide you sideways while held.
  Vision modes        N steps through the ones you have, then back to normal.
                      Thermal shows monsters and humans in hot colours against
                      cold buildings.


FLYING SAFELY
-------------

Flying into a building stops you dead and damages the VTOL: the faster you
were going, the worse it is. A bump at low speed does no harm.


SAVING
------

Pause with BREAK, then press S to save and quit. There's one save game, and
saving again replaces it. Continue on the main menu brings it back, at the
start of the wave you were on.
